Barricade Math Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

The short version: Barricade Math does not collect any information about you or your child. Everything the app remembers stays on your device.

Who we are

Barricade Math is made by Falling Sausage, a small family game studio. You can reach us at hello@fallingsausage.com.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no forms. It never asks for a name, an age, an email address, a photo, a location or contacts, and it does not use the camera or the microphone.

What the app remembers, and where

To pick the right questions, the app keeps a practice record on the device it is installed on:

  • which math facts have been practiced, and whether each answer was right or wrong;
  • how long answers took, which the app uses to tell when a fact is well known;
  • a list of past practice sessions;
  • the settings you chose, such as which tables to practice and whether sound is on.

This record is stored only on that device. The app never sends it to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it.

The app does not use the internet

Barricade Math works fully offline and contains no code that connects to the internet. It has:

  • no advertising;
  • no analytics or tracking;
  • no software from other companies;
  • no in-app purchases;
  • no links to websites, social media or other apps.

Children

Barricade Math is made for children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children, including children under 13.

Deleting the practice record

You can erase it at any time. In the app, open Progress and choose "Reset all progress". Deleting the app from the device also erases it.

Device backups

If you back up your device with iCloud or to a computer, Apple's backup may include the app's practice record along with your other app data. That backup is yours, under your Apple account. We never receive it.

What Apple may tell us

Apple runs the App Store. If the owner of a device has chosen to share analytics with app developers in the device's settings, Apple may show us combined, anonymous figures, such as how many times the app was downloaded or how often it crashed. These figures do not identify anyone, and the app itself plays no part in gathering them. Apple's own privacy policy covers what Apple collects.

This website

This website does not use cookies or trackers. It is hosted by GitHub, which, like most web hosts, may record technical details such as the IP address of visitors. GitHub's privacy statement covers that.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and change the date at the top before the new version is released.

Questions
