About the game

Barricade Math is a math facts game for kids about 8 to 11. It covers addition, subtraction, multiplication and division with the numbers 1 to 12. Goofy zombies shuffle toward your house, and right answers keep the barricade strong. Nobody gets hurt: the zombies just get sent home.

The game works without the internet. It has no ads, no purchases and no accounts.

Playing

How do I play?

Pick what you want to practice, tap Start, type each answer on the number pad and tap Go. "How to play" on the home screen has the full guide.

What happens when I get one wrong?

The game shows you the right answer and waits until you tap Next. That fact comes back a few questions later, carried by a zombie. Get it right and the zombie goes home. Each miss adds one question to the session.

What is the dance party?

If three zombies reach the barricade, they start dancing. Nothing bad happens, and the session carries on.

What is Zombie Rush?

An extra challenge you can switch on from the home screen. A zombie walks toward the barricade while you think. Running out of time never counts as a wrong answer. It is off unless you turn it on.

Why does it keep asking me some facts more than others?

The game works out which facts you find hard and practices those more, while mixing in ones you already know.

What do the colors on the Progress screen mean?

  • Gray: a fact you have not met yet.
  • Orange: still learning it.
  • Yellow: almost there.
  • Green: mastered, which means answered correctly and quickly on more than one day.

Tap any square to see how that fact is going.

Settings

How do I turn the sound off?

Use the Sound switch on the home screen. The game also goes quiet when your iPhone's ring/silent switch is set to silent.

How do I start over?

Open Progress, scroll to the bottom and tap "Reset all progress". This erases everything the game has remembered and cannot be undone.

Can two children share one device?

The game keeps one practice record per device, so two players would share it. Separate players are not supported yet.

Troubleshooting

My progress is gone.

Progress is stored only on the device. It is erased if the app is deleted or if "Reset all progress" is used. It does not move between devices on its own.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or later.

Something looks wrong or the app closed by itself.

Please email us and tell us what you were doing, which device you have, and what you saw. A screenshot helps a lot.
